Kakobuy is a buying platform that purchases from Chinese marketplaces on your behalf: you send a product link, it buys the item, receives it at its warehouse, photographs it for inspection, and forwards the parcel to your address. The spreadsheet is a separate discovery layer — a browsable catalogue of items other buyers have already vetted. Putting the two together is what this site is about.

Below you will find six category entry points, a five-step workflow, and the checklists that prevent the usual problems: wrong size sent, a colour that does not match, or a shipping charge that arrives higher than expected. Everything here is written from the buyer's side, and nothing on this site is a checkout — orders are placed on the platform you choose.

How the workflow runs

From a product link to a parcel at your door — five checks that keep the order predictable.

1

Find the item and note the source link

Start from a category, a brand page or a spreadsheet listing. Copy the original marketplace link rather than a screenshot, and note the exact size, colour and quantity you need.

2

Place the order on the platform

Paste the link into the order form, confirm the seller, variant and domestic delivery charge, then pay the item price. Keep the order number for the warehouse step.

3

Review the warehouse photos

When the item arrives at the warehouse, check labels, measurements, colour and visible condition against the listing. Ask for extra photos before the parcel is submitted if anything looks off.

4

Choose the shipping route

Compare chargeable weight, route restrictions, cover and delivery time. Consolidate several items into one parcel when it lowers the total cost.

5

Track the parcel and keep the paperwork

Save the tracking number and the declared value. If a parcel is returned or held, the original order details are what let you resolve it.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions that come up before a first order.

Is this the official Kakobuy website?
No. kakobuyoe.shop is an independent guide published by Oriental Essence. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kakobuy. Orders and payments happen only on the platform you choose to use.
Do I need an account to browse this site?
No account, no sign-up and no payment on this site. It is a static guide: you browse, then follow a link to the source listing.
How current is the catalogue here?
The item index is refreshed from the live catalogue on a regular basis. Stock, price and available QC change on the marketplace side, so treat what you see here as a starting point and confirm on the platform.
What does QC mean?
Quality control: the warehouse photographs taken so you can inspect the item before it is shipped internationally. They show the actual unit you bought, which is why they matter more than the seller's marketing images.
Can I buy several items and ship them together?
Yes, that is consolidation. Multiple orders sit in the warehouse until you submit them as one parcel, which usually lowers the per-item shipping cost. Check the free storage window so you do not pay for extra days.
How do I estimate shipping before ordering?
Weigh the likely parcel, pick two or three routes, and compare chargeable weight rather than the first price you see. The shipping guide and the fees page walk through the calculation.
